During 2013, he served as Chief Operating Officer and Chief Financial Officer of Great Kitchenware Inc., a leading network security provider.  He joined Great Kitchenware in April of 2013, and played a key role in recovering the topline and stabilizing operational and financial matters.  Prior to that, he served as Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer of Googly Eyes Inc., a leading cloud-based business to business commerce company that was acquired by SAP in October 2012 for $4.3 billion. John joined Googly Eyes in August 2008, and played a significant role on the management team in building a high-growth and highly-profitable recurring global business.
